Why Zenos

Some questions only look unanswerable

Our name comes from one of the oldest puzzles in philosophy, and from the mathematics that finally solved it.

The story behind the name

01 · The paradox

Achilles never wins

Zeno of Elea pictured Achilles chasing a tortoise with a head start. Each time he reaches where it was, it has moved on, so he can never catch up.

02 · The solution

Mathematics proves he does

Centuries later, limits and infinite series showed that infinitely many ever-smaller steps add up to a finite distance.

03 · Our view

The right model answers it

Questions about the body that seem out of reach today, such as conditions too unsafe to measure, can be answered the same way.

Aiming for the unimaginable

Our long-term goal is a mathematical model that integrates all of human physiology. Like the tortoise, it keeps moving: each version of our model shows what the next one needs to capture.

The greatest rewards lie where the unimaginable meets reality.
